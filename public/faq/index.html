How do I start?

Choose a build mode, add a required skill, select its level, and Find builds. Try the comfort example for Stun Resistance and Evade Extender.

Does craftable mode mean no talisman?

No. It equips one craftable talisman or no talisman, whichever fits. Owned mode searches only your owned talismans. No result combines two talismans.

Can I use weapon skills on a talisman?

Yes. The library preserves weapon and armor skills and a separate weapon-decoration slot. Enter all attributes as shown in your game. Unknown IDs are retained in storage but must be resolved before searching.

What do decoration quantities do?

Without quantities, selected types have unlimited copies. Enable Use only quantities I own to enforce actual stock. Empty or zero counts are unavailable, including in leftover-slot suggestions.

Why are some decorations hidden?

Weapon jewels are shown when your inputs involve weapon skills or weapon slots. Armor jewels cannot be placed in weapon slots, or vice versa.

Why are there no results?

Check required levels, quantities, locks, exclusions, rank and rarity. You can allow missing levels to inspect incomplete builds; they are labeled clearly. Search failures and unavailable data are reported separately.

What does preferred mean?

Preferred skills are optional. The search preserves required-target priority and favors builds and decoration assignments that cover more preferred levels.

Does the defense number include Defense Boost or food?

No. It is an equipment sum, using database maximum or base defense. Skill effects, meals and conditional bonuses are excluded.

Are theoretical talismans guaranteed to exist?

No. Theorycraft explores a sample of requirement templates; legal combinations and probabilities are not verified. Use owned mode for an actual talisman.

How are Gogma weapons modeled?

Enter the actual set roll and group roll independently in My weapon and Gogma bonuses, together with the weapon's slots and innate skills. The app does not guess a legal roll pair.

Can I use transcended armor?

At HR100+, enter the piece's actual defense and slots from your game. The API does not expose verified automatic transcended variants. Base mode ignores entered upgrade overrides.

Can I recover an edited or cleared library?

Editing keeps the original until Save Changes. Imports and deletes retain the previous inventory; use Restore previous inventory to undo the most recent change. Export a JSON backup for longer-term recovery.

What is shared in a link?

Settings links contain search inputs. Exact-build links also contain the equipped talisman, weapon inputs, placements and database version. Your full inventory is not shared. Older query/hash settings links remain readable, with legacy controls mapped to current behavior.

Does the database version match the game patch?

Not necessarily. A database timestamp and a game patch number are different. Inspect Game data and update coverage for source/freshness and current verification limits.

Will Ascendance builds be available?

Expansion integration requires released, verified equipment and rules. Preview announcements are not used as current calculations.
