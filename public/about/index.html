Find gear for the skills you want

Choose craftable talismans, your owned library, or exploratory theorycraft. Pick required and preferred skills, choose decorations and optional quantities, then Find builds.

What the search models

  • Five armor pieces and exactly one talisman, or no talisman.
  • Armor and weapon decoration slots, jewel quantities and exact placements.
  • Set/group piece thresholds, including independently entered Gogma weapon rolls.
  • Armor locks and exclusions, rank/rarity filters, base defense or supplied upgraded values.
  • Missing required levels and preferred skill gains, ranked consistently.

Using results

Results show the equipped talisman, target completion, active set/group skills, equipment defense and resistances. Expand decorations to see the exact loadout. Pin up to three builds to compare, save named builds on this device, export a checklist, or share an exact-build link.

What the numbers mean

This is a skill and equipment planner, not a weapon DPS calculator. Food, skill-driven defense/resistance effects, conditional effect uptime, monster hitzones, motion values and weapon-switch timing are excluded.

Data coverage

The game patch reviewed for changes is 1.042.00.02 (August 14, 2026). Database version and fetched time are separate. The public API does not certify complete patch coverage, expose verified transcended variants, or provide complete legal random-roll tables.

Enter actual weapon rolls and armor upgrade values from your game. Hypothetical talismans are clearly labeled unverified. Appraised talismans retain weapon skills and slots. Timeworn Charms improve rarity odds without changing the legal attributes.

Your data

Inventories and saved builds are stored in your browser. Export backups before changing devices. Imports retain a previous-inventory backup. Shared build links contain the selected talisman and build details, not the entire owned library.
